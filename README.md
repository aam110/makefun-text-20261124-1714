One resignation letter, exactly one line long, whose only job is to be funny.
Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The One-Line Exit"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-the-one-line-exit-1dc652d1).

> I resign as your mime after eleven years of silence: this letter is the loudest thing I have ever said at work.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the text to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The output is a single line of 111 characters, no trailing newline — inside the
competition gate of 1–140 characters of UTF-8 text.

## Why this line

A mime's whole job is silence, so a resignation letter — itself a silent piece of
paper — becomes the loudest thing he has ever said at work. The specific job
carries the joke, the turn lands in the last clause, and the line reads like a
letter a real person could actually send.
